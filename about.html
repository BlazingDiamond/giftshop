The story behind the shop

Made by hand,
given with heart

Welcome... I am so grateful your journey brought you here.

I’ve always believed that the most meaningful things in life aren’t rushed—they are created with time, intention, and love. That belief is woven into every handcrafted creation, natural product, and healing session offered here.

Working with nature’s gifts—crystals, botanicals, pure ingredients, and gentle energy—is my way of helping you slow down and re-center. Every item is thoughtfully chosen and crafted in small batches to nurture, comfort, and bring a touch of peace to your day.

For me, healing isn't about fixing anyone; it's about creating a safe, gentle space where you can breathe, feel supported, and reconnect with yourself. A quiet moment, a comforting aroma, or a caring touch can be powerful reminders to practice self-care.

This is more than a shop—it is a place where creativity meets compassion, and where every offering carries a piece of my heart. Whether you leave with a handmade treasure, a restorative treatment, or simply a moment of calm, my wish is that you feel a little lighter than when you arrived.

Thank you for supporting handmade, honoring a dream built on love, and walking this path with me. You are welcome here, exactly as you are.

Take a deep breath... you’re right where you need to be. 🤍

What we believe

Our values

Ethically sourced

Every crystal is traced back to a responsible supplier. Every plant ingredient comes from organic or wild-harvested sources. We know where our materials come from.

🤲

Truly handmade

Not "handmade by a team of 20." Made by one person. One at a time. This limits how much we can produce — and that's exactly the point.

Made with intention

We believe that energy transfers through touch. Every piece is made in a calm space, with attention and care. You'll feel the difference when it's in your hands.
